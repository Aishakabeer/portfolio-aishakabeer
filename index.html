About me

Engineer by training, business manager by responsibility, and teacher by habit.

I'm a software engineer and business manager based in Kottayam, Kerala. I completed my Master of Computer Applications at KTU University after a BCA at MG University, and I've been building software professionally since my first internship in 2023.

On the technical side I work with Python, Django, React, Node.js, PHP and MySQL, and I've used Azure and AWS for cloud work. I've built a tax-compliant e-invoicing system that signs XML invoices and submits them to a government API, a live waste-management booking platform with real-time tracking, and a construction planning system with budget modules.

On the business side I coordinate teams, manage projects, look after client and institutional relationships, and support sales and business development. Working in both worlds means I can translate between clients and developers, keep delivery on schedule, and make decisions that are good for the product and for the organization.

I also train professionals in AI, ML and modern full-stack development, using hands-on problem-solving and collaborative learning. I'm most interested in strategic planning, team leadership, operational improvement and finding new business opportunities that technology can unlock.

From Zero to AI Executive: AI at Work and on LinkedIn

A session in a one-day AI tools workshop on bringing AI into everyday work and into a stronger LinkedIn profile.

On 28 September 2026 I took a session in "From Zero to AI Executive", a one-day workshop on AI tools hosted by the Department of Commerce together with the IQAC at Rajagiri Viswajyothi College of Arts and Applied Sciences, Vengoor, Perumbavoor. My part focused on putting AI to work in real tasks and connecting that work to LinkedIn.

For commerce students, AI can help with research, drafting reports, preparing presentations and making sense of data. I showed how this looks in practice with demonstrations on the classroom screen, and I stressed that every output should be checked and made your own before it is used.

We then linked that work to LinkedIn: using AI to draft and sharpen a headline and About section, and to describe projects and skills clearly, while keeping the voice honest and personal. The students followed closely and asked good questions, and I'm grateful to the Department of Commerce, the IQAC and the college for the invitation.

Zero to Pro: LinkedIn and Web Dev with AI

A session for B.Tech Computer Science students on building a professional LinkedIn profile and learning web development with AI tools.

On 25 September 2026 I led a "Zero to Pro" session for B.Tech Computer Science students at Mangalam College of Engineering, Ettumanoor. The idea was simple: show what a student starting from zero can do, with LinkedIn and with web development, to become visible and job-ready before graduation.

In the web development part I showed how AI tools can help write, explain and debug code and speed up building pages from an idea. I stressed using them to learn faster rather than to skip the learning: read the code it gives you, understand it, and be able to explain it in an interview.

For LinkedIn we covered how to build a profile from scratch, with a clear headline, a focused About section, and skills and projects that show what you can do. The students were attentive and asked good questions, and I'm grateful to the college for another great session.

Web Development Meets AI: Tools and Career Paths for Polytechnic Students

A session on using AI tools to build for the web faster, followed by practical guidance on choosing a career path in tech.

On 25 September 2026 I visited Mangalam Polytechnic College, Ettumanoor, for a session on web development with AI tools and a career guidance section. For many of these students, web development is one of the most direct routes into a tech career, and AI tools have made the first steps much easier.

In the web development part I showed how AI tools can help write and explain HTML, CSS and JavaScript, fix bugs, and speed up building a page from an idea. The key message was to use AI as a coach, not a shortcut: read what it produces, understand it, and be able to explain your own code.

Work Smarter, Get Noticed: AI Tools and LinkedIn for Future Managers

A session for MBA students on using AI tools in everyday management work and building a LinkedIn profile that opens doors.

Build Smarter, Ship Faster: The AI Toolkit for Developers

A full day with MCA students on the AI tools that are changing how software is written, tested and presented.

On 11 September 2026 I spent a full day with MCA students at Mangalam College of Engineering, Ettumanoor, teaching the AI toolkit for developers. A whole day meant we could go beyond a quick tour of names and actually work with the tools, one after another.

We looked at the AI tools that are changing how software gets built: coding agents that help write, explain and review code, models that can produce 3D content, and AI video creation. Students followed along on the lab computers and tried each idea for themselves, so every demonstration turned into practice rather than just a slide.

One part of the day focused on keeping reports and project work original. We talked about using AI as a helper without letting it replace your own thinking, and how plagiarism and AI checks work. The class stayed curious from morning to evening, and I'm grateful to Mangalam College of Engineering and the MCA students for their questions and energy.

From Data to Decisions: AI-Powered Excel, Power BI and LinkedIn

A workshop on using AI tools inside Excel and Power BI, finished with a practical look at optimizing a LinkedIn profile.

On 18 August 2026 I was at KMEA College of Arts & Science, Kuzhivelippady, Edathala, for a business analytics workshop hosted by the Department of Management together with the IQAC. The focus was on bringing AI into the tools students already use, Excel and Power BI, so that analysing data becomes faster and easier to understand.

The hands-on part showed how AI tools can support everyday analytics work: writing and explaining formulas, cleaning and organizing raw data, spotting patterns, and turning numbers into clear Power BI reports. Students worked through examples on their own computers, so each technique was tried immediately rather than just described.

We closed with LinkedIn optimization. We covered how to write a strong headline and About section, present skills and projects clearly, and use the profile to get noticed by recruiters. Because students had just seen what they can do with data, it was a natural way to show how to present those skills. The room was attentive and asked plenty of questions, and I'm grateful to the Department of Management, the IQAC and the students for a great session.

AI tools and practical experience

A three-hour, hands-on session where students stopped hearing about AI and started using it, one computer at a time.

On 30 July 2026 I led a three-hour session on AI tools and practical experience at KMM College of Arts and Science, Thrikkakara. The goal was simple: move past talking about AI and give students the chance to use it themselves, in a real computer lab, on real tasks.

We started with a short introduction to what modern AI tools can and cannot do, then spent most of the time on hands-on practice. Every student worked at their own computer while I walked between the rows, answered questions and helped anyone who got stuck. The focus was on asking clearer questions, checking what the tools produce, and applying the results to study and project work.

The three hours went really well. The room stayed engaged from start to finish, questions kept coming, and students left with practical habits they can use straight away. Thank you to the college and to the students for their energy and curiosity.

A session that helped students see their career options and start building a professional presence on LinkedIn.
